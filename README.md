## The line

> I asked my GPS for the meaning of life; it said 'recalculating' — honestly, the most honest answer anyone's ever given me.

The joke is a question nobody can answer, asked to the one machine whose entire job is knowing where you're going — and its answer is the most honest thing in the room: it doesn't know either, it's just recalculating. The punchline lands inside the line, needs no setup, and the image of a GPS admitting existential defeat is easy to repeat to someone else.
